To My Dearest Ankshika,

"The smile I broke, and the smile I will spend my life working to earn back."

Ankshika, I am writing this with a heavy heart, tears in my eyes, and a depth of remorse that words can hardly hold. I want to look you in the eyes—even through these words—and say plainly what I did, without hiding behind any veil: I betrayed your trust. I cheated on you, and I broke the sacred bond we spent so long building.

I want to make something unequivocally clear right now: I offer zero excuses. I will not blame distance, I will not blame stress, I will not blame alcohol, and I will never dare to blame any argument or misunderstanding between us. What happened was the result of my own weakness, extreme selfishness, and a catastrophic lapse in character. You did nothing—absolutely nothing—to cause or deserve this agony. The guilt, the shame, and the responsibility rest entirely on my shoulders alone.

“

You gave me your precious heart, your vulnerability, your late-night calls, your sleepy laughter, and your faith. And instead of protecting that sacred gift, I carelessly shattered it. Seeing you in pain because of my actions is the most unbearable realization of my life.

I know that the hardest part of betrayal is not just the act itself, but the cruel robbery of your peace. It makes you question every memory we ever shared, every sweet thing I ever whispered, and every plan we made for tomorrow. You do not deserve to lie awake at night wondering why you weren't enough. You were always more than enough, Ankshika. You have been the brightest, kindest, most extraordinary light in my life, and my failure was entirely my own inner sickness, never yours.

I am not writing this to emotionally blackmail you into easing my guilt, or to force you to smile and say "it’s okay." It is not okay. You have every right to feel furious, disappointed, disgusted, heartbroken, or completely numb. Every single emotion you feel right now is 100% valid, and I will sit in whatever fire you need me to sit in without ever getting defensive or asking you to "move on faster."

I want you to know with absolute certainty: There is no one else I want. There never was anyone who could compare to you, and there never will be. The thought of a life without you is hollow, but more than my own loneliness, my priority is your healing. Even if healing takes time, even if it requires hard conversations, even if you need to yell at me or ask me questions a thousand times—I am here, Ankshika. I am not running away.

I love you with all the broken pieces of my humbled heart. I am deeply, devastatingly sorry.

Always and forever accountable to you,

Ujjawal

Actionable Repentance

Concrete Commitments to Rebuild What I Broke

Apologies mean nothing without sustained, verifiable behavioral change. These are not empty wishes; they are the non-negotiable standards I hold myself to from this moment onward.

1. Radical & Unrestricted Transparency

I surrender any expectation of secretive privacy between us. My phone, messages, social accounts, and location are open to you whenever you want, without you ever having to feel guilty for checking. Transparency is no longer a privilege—it is the floor of our relationship.

Open Devices • Zero Hidden Accounts • Proactive Updates

2. Defenseless & Unflinching Honesty

Whenever you have questions—whether right now, at 3:00 AM, or six months down the line—I will answer every single detail with complete, unedited truth. I will never say "can we not talk about this again," roll my eyes, or act frustrated. You deserve clarity, not minimization.

No Gaslighting • Full Accountability • Infinite Patience

3. Respecting Your Timeline & Boundaries

I will never rush your healing process. If you need days of silence, I will respect that. If you need physical distance, I will step back while remaining readily reachable. You hold the steering wheel; I will follow the pace that feels safe for your heart.

Your Terms • Zero Pressure • Patient Consistency

4. Genuine Inner Work & Personal Counseling

I recognize that this betrayal stemmed from deep personal flaws, validation-seeking, or lack of discipline within me. I am committing to individual therapy / counseling and conscious self-discipline so that I become the grounded, trustworthy partner you always deserved.

Therapy / Inner Healing • Fixing Root Causes • Real Growth

5. Severing All Toxic Doors & Temptations

I have permanently severed contact, blocked, and removed any individual or environment associated with this betrayal. There will be zero ambiguity or blurred lines in who has access to me. My emotional and physical presence belongs solely to you.

Permanently Blocked • Zero Contact • Ironclad Boundaries

6. Never in Shadows: Privacy Without Secrecy

I know you want our bond to be public, and I understand why: after what I did, secrecy feels dangerous and terrifying. I promise: keeping our healing sacred between us will never mean hiding you or acting single. Anyone who matters in my life will know without a doubt that my heart and loyalty belong solely to you, Ankshika.

Zero Acting Single • Proud of You • Healing Our Foundation First

7. Re-Earning Your Trust Brick by Brick

I understand that trust isn't restored by an apology; it is slowly re-built through hundreds of quiet, consistent, honest days. I am prepared to put in the quiet work for as long as it takes, asking for nothing in return except the chance to prove my devotion.

Long-Term Devotion • Deeds Over Words • Steadfast Loyalty

My Day-to-Day Actions Starting Today

  • Check-ins without smothering: Letting you know where I am and what I am doing so you never have to wonder or worry.
  • Emotional safety first: When you feel triggered or sad, my only response will be comfort, listening, and holding space—never irritation.
  • Active appreciation: Remembering and honoring every single reason you are irreplaceable to me.
